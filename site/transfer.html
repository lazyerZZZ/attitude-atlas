Which early switches helped?

On the five-minute trace, three of five early switches prevented a budget breach on at least one of the next two eligible windows. For the other two switches the previous fit stayed within budget throughout that follow-up. On the ten-minute trace, one of four switches prevented a breach and the other three had a previous fit still within budget. These counts use the retired fit on the same future observations.

False alarms and missed changes

Known-label controls keep the recorded sampling schedule and replace ROS time with a specified clock signal. At two onset times we tested stable clocks, short pulses, sustained steps and changes in rate. Detection had a 40-second deadline. Each policy had 28 trials with insufficient coverage.
