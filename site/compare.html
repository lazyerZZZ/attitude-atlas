How the comparison works

Both rotations are expressed in geographic ENU with a forward-left-up body. The full rotation error is the smallest angle aligning them, from 0° to 180°. Nose direction compares only their forward axes. The clock offset uses PX4 timestamp_sample when available, falling back to timestamp, and ROS header.stamp. A zero stamp is treated as unavailable. A time offset or an unaligned map can explain an angular difference.
