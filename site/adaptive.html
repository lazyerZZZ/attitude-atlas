When to switch clock calibration

At 30–40 seconds on a VOXL 2 recording, the active fit had a 1.533 ms P95 error. Its replacement reached 0.137 ms. The early policy rejected that fit because it required 0.125 ms. Over the next ten seconds the old fit reached 2.365 ms.

What changed

The adaptive policy can nominate a new fit at half the budget. Two successive windows of growing signed error can confirm a rate change. A flat displacement needs three consecutive same-sign median errors over budget. A replacement must meet the full budget and halve P95 on a later window. The switch takes effect on subsequent events.

One real forward jump

On Delft flight vb_003, adjacent matched events advanced 51.007 ms on PX4 and 2,703.810 ms on ROS. The table includes this jump. The following 296.70 seconds form a continuous run on both clocks. A separate adaptive fit on that run scored 4,036 events at 0.362 ms P95 and left 385 events unscored during training or incomplete coverage.
