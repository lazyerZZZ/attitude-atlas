Capture formats and matching rules

Export vehicle_attitude with ulog2csv -m vehicle_attitude flight.ulg. Capture a stamped ROS orientation such as Odometry with ros2 topic echo /odom --full-length > ros.yaml. Stop the capture with Ctrl+C. Use one aligned frame pair throughout.

Each ROS header stamp is paired with the nearest PX4 timestamp within your chosen skew. Ties select the earlier PX4 sample. One PX4 sample may serve multiple ROS messages. The percentile uses nearest rank over matched ROS samples. With incomplete PX4 sample stamps, the whole comparison uses publication time.
